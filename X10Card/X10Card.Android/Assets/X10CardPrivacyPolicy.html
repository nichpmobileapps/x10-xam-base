Privacy Policy – “X10 Card” Mobile App

National Informatics Centre, Himachal Pradesh has Developed “X10 Card” Mobile App for Labour and Employment Department, Himachal Pradesh. The X10 Card application is useful for the unemployed youth who are registered with the various Employment Exchanges of Himachal Pradesh. The user needs to enter his/her Registration Number (as in Employment Exchange Record) along with part of name and Date of Birth and the details of registrant are available. These details remain available in off-line mode too. The applicant can view the relevant vacancy information, if available, matching his/her qualifications. The user can also update his/her employment status. The user also gets renewal status of registration. The app is available on Play Store free of cost.
